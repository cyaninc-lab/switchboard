## Switchboard 0.1.21

- **Use Switchboard from your phone's browser.** Each machine now serves the app at its own address. With "Reachable from my other devices over Tailscale" on (Settings), open `http://<machine>:7878/` on your phone (on Tailscale) and pair it with a code from Settings → Pair another device.
- **A phone layout.** On a phone: your sessions full screen with Sessions / Inbox / Settings at the bottom, and chats like text messages (you on the right, Claude on the left, its steps as small cards), with a Send button.
- First step towards the Switchboard phone app.
