## Switchboard 0.1.33

- **Reach your machines from anywhere, through any VPN:** Settings → Account → Sign in with GitHub. Each of your devices signs in once; the first with a screen is trusted, later ones wait until you approve them (Settings → Account, or the "wants to join" button at the top). Your machines then appear on every device by themselves, and removing a device cuts it off at once. No Tailscale needed.
- **Settings is tidier:** tabs (Appearance, Account, Sessions, Machines, Notifications, About), one machine picker, short explanations (hover or tap for more). Pairing a device moved to the machine's name in the top bar.
- Fix (phone): an update installs only when you tap "ready", and only a newer version.
