## Switchboard 0.1.24

- **The phone app (Android)** is now attached to each release as `Switchboard.apk`: download it on your phone and install. Pair it by scanning the QR code in Settings → Pair another device. It updates itself: when a new version is out it offers "Download and install".
- **Phone notifications** when a session needs you, finishes, or a watch fires: Allow / Deny right from the notification, or reply to Claude without opening the app. They come through your own [ntfy](https://ntfy.sh) server, no Google account needed: set its address once in Settings → Phone notifications (on any machine) and the phone app does the rest.
- What the lock screen shows follows your phone's own setting (Notifications → hide sensitive content).
