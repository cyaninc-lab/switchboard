## Switchboard 0.1.16

- **Tabs.** Open several sessions side by side. Clicking a session opens a preview tab that the next click replaces; sending a message or double-clicking keeps it, and Ctrl+click opens a new one. Ctrl+Tab switches, Ctrl+W closes, drag to reorder. Each tab keeps its own draft and scroll position, and your tabs come back after a restart.
- **Pop out a session into its own window.** Use **Pop out** in the session header, or right-click → Open in new window. Its draft goes with it. Use **Keep on top** to keep it above other windows; closing the window puts it back as a tab.
- **Hide the session list.** The button left of "Switchboard" folds it away at any size, and it folds by itself when the window is narrow. While it's hidden, the button still shows when a session needs you.
- **Close idle sessions, if you want.** Settings → Idle sessions: per machine, close a session after it's been quiet for a while, to free memory. It's off unless you turn it on, and sessions that are busy or waiting on you are never closed.
- **Version check between machines.** A machine's chip now says when it runs an older or newer Switchboard than this one, so you know which to update.
