## Switchboard 0.1.14

- **Your messages stand out.** What you typed now has a soft coloured band down its side, so it's easy to spot when scrolling back through a long session.
- **Web links open in your browser.** Clicking a link in a reply, the inbox or a question now opens it in your default browser instead of inside the Switchboard window.
- **File names in replies are clickable.** When Claude mentions a file or folder (like `server/app.py`), click it: folders open in your file manager and documents, images and text open in their usual app. Scripts and programs are never run: they're only shown in their folder. For a session on another machine, the path is copied instead.
