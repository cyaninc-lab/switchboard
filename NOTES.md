## Switchboard 0.1.23

- **Commands look like little terminal windows**: coloured dots and "Bash" in the title bar, then `$ the command` and its output.
- **Pair a phone by QR code.** Settings → Pair another device now shows a QR code next to the code. Scan it with any phone camera to open Switchboard there already paired (or with the new phone app, coming soon).
- **Background tasks:** the slim bar only shows while something runs; tap it (or ⋯ → Background tasks) to see running and finished ones. A finished task's notice shows as a step, not as if you'd sent it.
- **On a phone:** streamed replies grow in their bubble instead of jumping into it, images keep their shape, square send/attach/interrupt buttons, a short "working" hint, and the side chat takes the whole screen.
